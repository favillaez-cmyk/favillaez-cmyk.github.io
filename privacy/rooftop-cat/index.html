Rooftop Cat Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026. Applies to Rooftop Cat version 0.3.0, Android package com.favillaezlabs.rooftopcat, published by Favillaez Labs.

Information handled by the game

Rooftop Cat does not collect or send personal information, gameplay information, or device identifiers to Favillaez Labs or other companies. The game has no user accounts, advertising, analytics, in-app purchases, chat, or online leaderboards. Its game assets are bundled with the app.

Local score and device storage

The game saves your personal best score in local app storage so it can show your record between sessions. Favillaez Labs does not receive this score and does not operate a server holding your game data. The app allows Android backup; depending on your device and backup settings, Android or your device provider may back up and restore app data under their own policies.

Permissions and third-party code

The Android app declares Internet permission for its embedded web runtime. This version does not use it to send gameplay or personal information to an external server. Rooftop Cat uses Capacitor and Three.js to run and display the game; it does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location.

Children and families

Rooftop Cat is intended for players of all ages, including children. The game does not collect personal information from children or offer advertising, purchases, user accounts, or communication between players. A parent or guardian may contact us with privacy questions.

Deleting local data

To erase your saved score, clear Rooftop Cat's app storage in Android Settings. Uninstalling removes local app data, although a device backup may restore it on reinstallation. Manage any backup through your Android or device-provider settings. There is no game account to delete.

Support email

If you choose to email favillaezlabs@gmail.com, we receive the email address, message, and any attachments you send and use them to respond to your request. This is separate from the game, which does not send support messages automatically. Email is handled through Gmail under Google's Privacy Policy. Do not send sensitive information. You may request deletion of support correspondence at the same address; legal obligations and email-provider backup processes may affect deletion.

Google Play and this website

Google Play, Android, and your device provider may independently process information when you download, update, back up, or use your device, under their own privacy policies. This policy describes the game's own behavior. This privacy page is hosted on GitHub Pages; see our website privacy information for hosting details.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy and its effective date if the game's privacy practices change. For questions about this policy or support correspondence, contact Favillaez Labs at favillaezlabs@gmail.com.
